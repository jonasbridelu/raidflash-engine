# CAKE × RAIDFLASH

CAKE answers people, not tenants. A clinic, a researcher, a student with
one box: they bought a GPU. The stack gave them C1. The same silicon does
4–20× in the vendor's multi-user bench.

RAIDFLASH is the occupancy layer so CAKE spends **the whole die** on the
person in front of it — or splits that full die toward whoever needs
compute now.

v0.1's tools are crude (seed, sisters, an EFI map). That is fine. The
community will sharpen them. The market shift is simpler: you do not have
to buy another GPU to go faster if the one you own was sitting at 10%.
Sovereign AI. Local AI. Always try for 100% of the chip you paid for.

- P: seed the record, then sister stripes (prefix cache)
- D: dense decode on those lanes until the knee
- Injector: `k = min(PARALLEL, k_codo)` per user, per phase

Open-sourcing 0.1 is how ordinary users help make CAKE honest about tok/s.

CAKE.
